# Overview  
This document summarizes the key insights derived from the Inventory Analytics Dashboard and outlines recommended actions for inventory, supplier, manufacturing, and logistics optimization.  
Insights are based on trends, KPIs, exception reports, and drill‑through analysis within the Power BI model.

---

# 1. Inventory Insights & Actions

## Insight 1 — High Inventory Value Concentrated in a Small Set of SKUs  
ABC analysis shows that ~15% of SKUs represent over 70% of total inventory value.

**Actions:**  
- Prioritize forecasting accuracy for A‑class SKUs.  
- Implement tighter reorder point controls for high‑value items.  
- Review carrying costs and evaluate opportunities to reduce excess stock.

---

## Insight 2 — Stockout Events Driven by Lead Time Variability  
Stockout heatmaps indicate recurring shortages for SKUs supplied by vendors with inconsistent lead times.

**Actions:**  
- Increase safety stock for SKUs with high lead time volatility.  
- Negotiate improved delivery commitments with affected suppliers.  
- Introduce supplier performance scorecards to monitor reliability.

---

## Insight 3 — Overstock Conditions in Slow‑Moving SKUs  
Overstock rate is elevated for products with declining demand and long replenishment cycles.

**Actions:**  
- Reduce reorder quantities for slow‑moving SKUs.  
- Run targeted promotions to clear excess inventory.  
- Evaluate SKU rationalization for low‑profit, low‑velocity items.

---

## Insight 4 — DOH Trending Upward  
Days of Inventory on Hand has increased over the last three months, indicating slower turnover.

**Actions:**  
- Reassess demand planning assumptions.  
- Align purchasing frequency with updated sales trends.  
- Review warehouse capacity constraints for high‑DOH categories.

---

# 2. Supplier Insights & Actions

## Insight 5 — Supplier Lead Time Variability Causing Replenishment Delays  
Lead time box plots show significant variability for several key suppliers.

**Actions:**  
- Implement supplier SLAs with penalties for late deliveries.  
- Shift volume to more reliable suppliers where feasible.  
- Increase visibility by requesting weekly production updates.

---

## Insight 6 — High Defect Rates Concentrated in Specific Product Lines  
Defect rate trends highlight quality issues in certain manufacturing batches.

**Actions:**  
- Conduct root‑cause analysis with suppliers.  
- Increase inspection frequency for high‑risk SKUs.  
- Adjust safety stock to account for expected scrap rates.

---

## Insight 7 — Supplier Scorecard Identifies Top and Bottom Performers  
Composite scoring reveals clear gaps in supplier reliability and quality.

**Actions:**  
- Reward high‑performing suppliers with preferred status.  
- Develop corrective action plans for low‑performing suppliers.  
- Consider dual‑sourcing for critical SKUs.

---

# 3. Manufacturing Insights & Actions

## Insight 8 — Production Bottlenecks Impacting Throughput  
Manufacturing throughput trends show reduced output during peak demand periods.

**Actions:**  
- Rebalance production schedules to match demand cycles.  
- Evaluate staffing or equipment constraints.  
- Increase preventive maintenance on high‑load production lines.

---

## Insight 9 — Rising Manufacturing Cost per Unit  
CPU has increased due to lower production volumes and higher defect rates.

**Actions:**  
- Improve quality control to reduce scrap.  
- Optimize batch sizes to reduce overhead allocation.  
- Review supplier material costs for negotiation opportunities.

---

## Insight 10 — First Pass Yield Declining  
FPY trends indicate growing quality issues.

**Actions:**  
- Implement additional in‑process inspections.  
- Review operator training and standard work procedures.  
- Conduct capability studies on critical machines.

---

# 4. Logistics Insights & Actions

## Insight 11 — On‑Time Delivery % Below Target  
Carrier performance analysis shows late deliveries on specific routes.

**Actions:**  
- Reevaluate carrier contracts and service levels.  
- Shift shipments to carriers with higher reliability.  
- Optimize route planning to reduce transit time variability.

---

## Insight 12 — Transportation Cost per Unit Increasing  
Shipping cost trends show rising expenses for air and expedited modes.

**Actions:**  
- Increase use of ground or consolidated shipments.  
- Improve demand forecasting to reduce emergency orders.  
- Negotiate volume‑based discounts with carriers.

---

## Insight 13 — Route Efficiency Gaps  
Route analysis reveals inefficient paths with high cost‑to‑distance ratios.

**Actions:**  
- Redesign routes to minimize distance and cost.  
- Evaluate cross‑dock opportunities.  
- Implement dynamic routing for high‑volume lanes.

---

# Summary  
The Inventory Analytics Platform provides actionable insights across inventory, supplier performance, manufacturing efficiency, and logistics operations.  
By executing the recommended actions, the organization can:

- Reduce stockouts and overstock  
- Improve supplier reliability  
- Increase manufacturing throughput  
- Lower transportation costs  
- Strengthen overall supply chain resilience
